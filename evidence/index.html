Scope and limitations

Navigation, marketplace filtering/pagination, product inquiry context, form validation and mock payloads, benchmark metrics/filters, keyboard access, and logo accessibility were checked. Tests use Chromium viewport simulation; Safari, Firefox, physical devices, and screen-reader speech were not tested.

Scores and catalogue metadata are snapshots. Scientific reports, legal pages, live job listings, and expert applications link to official sites. The frontend is a local reconstruction; no production backend is connected, no sales request or application was sent, no account was created, and nothing was deployed.
